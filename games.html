Games

  • Interstellar Escape

    Interstellar Escape is a story-driven arcade game I built with a team of interns through Games For Love. You play a space traveler stranded on an undiscovered planet, trying to find a way home. Each level is a brick-breaker challenge set by the planet's inhabitants, and short story scenes between levels reveal what happens next.

    Special tiles keep the brick-breaker levels from feeling repetitive, with effects ranging from changing the speed of the ball to warping it accross the field. The final level switches to an arcade-style boss fight where the player pilots their ship to avoid meteors and shoot down the boss.

    I contributed to general development, implemented and wrote the story, designed the menus and UI, and fixed bugs. I also helped coordinate communication between the development interns and other groups, such as the sound and art interns. The game was made in Unity with C#, using original assets created by the art interns.

  • Escape the Kitchen

    Made with Games For Love where I collaborated with a team of interns to create a 2-D platformer where the player plays as a chicken drumstick, and is trying to escape the kitchen. It was made in Unity using C#, and I implemented UI design, menus, and player interactability. There are 3 levels, with each one introducing a new mechanic. I worked in both script writing and design.

  • Forest Gnome

    Made with Games For Love where I collaborated with a team of interns to create a story 2-D platformer. The player plays as a forest gnome and must escape . They must face challenges put forth by he planet's inhabitants, in a brick breaker style mini game. Tiles have different effects such as slowing down the ball, shrinking or enlargening the player, and more. I implemented and wrote the story, designed the menus and UI, and performed bug fixes. This was made in Unity using C#. There are multiple levels with different gameplay mechanics, and that augment the story with player interaction.
